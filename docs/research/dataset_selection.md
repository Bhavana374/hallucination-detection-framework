## 1. Objective of Dataset Selection

The primary objective of this framework is to detect and classify **factual hallucinations** in Generative AI outputs using evidence grounding and multi-feature hybrid reasoning. To ensure academic rigor and reproducibility, candidate datasets must satisfy strict criteria:

1. **Relevance to Generative AI**: Must capture hallucination failure modes generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) and neural text generators.
2. **Grounding Evidence Availability**: Must provide either direct reference source passages, factual contexts, or allow extraction against a structured reference corpus.
3. **High-Quality Labeling**: Clear, consistent annotations distinguishing factual outputs from hallucinations / contradictions.
4. **Leakage-Safe Partitions**: Availability of distinct train, validation, and test splits without cross-sample contamination.
5. **Permissive Licensing**: Open research licensing (MIT, Apache 2.0, CC-BY-4.0) ensuring unencumbered academic use.

## 3. Selected Benchmark Strategy

To balance **Generative AI hallucination relevance** with **rigorous evidence-grounded NLI reasoning**, this framework adopts a **dual-tiered dataset strategy**:

### Tier 1: Primary Evaluation Benchmark — **HaluEval (QA & Summarization)**
- **Why Selected:** Specifically designed to evaluate LLM hallucination in question-answering and text generation tasks with paired factual context passages.
- **Dataset Structure:**
  - `query / instruction`: User prompt or question.
  - `grounding_evidence / context`: Factual passage from knowledge source.
  - `factual_response`: Verified factual response.
  - `hallucinated_response`: Perturbed/hallucinated response exhibiting entity swaps, fabricated facts, or logical contradictions.
- **Formulation:** Binary claim-level classification (`0: Factual`, `1: Hallucinated`).

### Tier 2: Evidence Grounding & NLI Calibration — **FEVER-aligned NLI Schema**
- **Why Selected:** Provides the gold standard 3-class semantic relationship required for the evidence-grounding module:
  - `0: Entailment (SUPPORTS)` → Evidence directly substantiates the claim.
  - `1: Contradiction (REFUTES)` → Evidence directly contradicts the claim.
  - `2: Neutral (NOT ENOUGH INFO)` → Evidence is irrelevant or insufficient to verify.

## 5. Safeguards Against Data Leakage

To preserve strict academic validity:
1. **Stratified Split Integrity**: The raw dataset is partitioned into $70\%$ Train, $15\%$ Validation, and $15\%$ Test using fixed pseudo-random seeds ($42$) with stratified label balances.
2. **Context & Evidence Separation**: Training sets and test sets share zero common prompt-response pairs.
3. **Feature Isolation**: No vectorizer (TF-IDF), scaler (StandardScaler), or semantic clustering index is fitted on the combined corpus. Fitting is strictly restricted to `train`, then transferred to `val` and `test`.
4. **Deduplication Audit**: Preprocessing checks exact and near-duplicate cosine overlap ($>0.95$) to prune duplicate claims across partitions.

---

## 6. Documented Limitations

- **Synthetic Distortions in Benchmarks**: A portion of HaluEval negative examples are generated via controlled LLM perturbation. While representative, human-in-the-loop adversarial attacks in open-domain chat may present subtle nuanced hallucinations.
- **Retrieval Scope**: Dense vector retrieval depends on the coverage of the grounding knowledge store; out-of-knowledge facts are categorized as `Neutral / Insufficient Evidence`.
